# AI Film Camp — initial logo concepts

## Brief

- Brand character: cinematic, innovative, fun, warm, and creative
- Primary visual territory: fire, lenses, and camera apertures
- Primary color: amber
- Logo type: standalone pictorial or abstract mark
- Priority uses: macOS app icon and website header
- Style: minimal and clean

## Palette

- Camp Amber: `#F6A21A`
- Ember Orange: `#E85D24`
- Firelight: `#F8BC4A`
- Charcoal: `#241C16`
- Warm Cream: `#FFF8ED`

The production SVGs use transparent backgrounds. The cream is only a presentation color.

## Concepts

### 01 — Lensfire

A direct fusion of a lens-shaped eye and a flame. It is the most immediately legible cinematic option and has a friendly, emblematic silhouette.

### 02 — Ember Iris

Six aperture blades orbit a centered flame. This direction emphasizes coordinated production and many moving pieces resolving around a creative idea.

### 03 — Focus Flame

A flame held by four camera focus corners. It is direct, playful, and especially well suited to a tool whose job is bringing a film project into focus.

### 04 — Shutter Blaze

Three fluid shutter blades combine into an abstract flame. This is the most ownable and least literal direction, trading some instant recognition for distinctiveness.

## Files and use

Each concept has three standalone SVGs:

- `-color.svg` — primary mark on light or neutral backgrounds
- `-dark.svg` — single-color charcoal mark on light backgrounds
- `-light.svg` — single-color white mark on dark backgrounds

The light variants have transparent backgrounds and may appear blank in viewers that use white canvases.

These are selection-stage concepts, not final production assets. After one direction is selected, refine its geometry and optical balance, test it in actual macOS app-icon masks and website-header layouts, establish clear space and minimum sizes, and export the final icon family.
