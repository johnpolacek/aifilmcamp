# AI Film Camp — Crescent Sweep refinement

This controlled study starts from the monochrome **Crescent Sweep** and progressively enlarges its lens ring while reducing its inner moon. The 130% Wider Still flame, ring stroke weight, color, and lens position remain fixed.

- **A — Gentle:** ring radius 36, moon radius 22
- **B — Balanced:** ring 37, moon 21
- **C — Airy:** ring 38, moon 20
- **D — Open:** ring 39, moon 19
- **E — Minimal:** ring 40, moon 18

All lens details are transparent knockouts. Each SVG is genuinely one-color and can be recolored without changing its construction.
