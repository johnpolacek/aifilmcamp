# AI Film Camp logo concepts — round 2

This round responds to the request for a cleaner, more modern mark.

## Reduction decisions

- One flat amber: `#F5A623`
- One neutral charcoal: `#191715`
- No gradients, shadows, outlines, inner-flame decoration, or fine detail
- Strong negative space and recognizable one-color silhouettes
- Geometry tested at compact website-header and app-icon glyph sizes

## Concepts

1. **Flare** — flame outside, circular lens inside. The most balanced combination of the requested visual ideas.
2. **Ember Disc** — lens outside, flame inside. The cleanest and most app-icon-ready direction.
3. **Camp Signal** — a literal campfire reduced to one flame and two rounded logs. The warmest and most accessible direction.
4. **Tri Iris** — three aperture blades in motion. The most abstract and technology-forward direction.

Each concept is provided as color, monochrome dark, and monochrome light SVG. All marks are standalone and use transparent backgrounds.
