AI Film Camp — four marks

Standalone symbols, no wordmark. Each is built from fire, lens and aperture geometry inside a 96-unit square, using one flat amber plus a neutral and letting negative space carry the idea. Every mark is shown in full colour, solid black, solid white, at 32/24/16 px, and on a dark app tile.

Amber #F5A524 Ink #1C1917 Cream #F7F3EE (neutral on dark)
01

Bokeh Sparks

Three embers lifting off a fire on a rising diagonal, cut as the hexagonal bokeh a lens makes of a point of light. Fire and optics are the same shape here: the spark is the aperture.

The most playful and the most modular — the three marks can animate, stack, or act as a bullet. Weakest single silhouette of the four.

02

Ember Arc

A lens ring caught mid-open. One blade has peeled away from the barrel and tapered into a single lick of flame, so the ring and the fire are one unbroken stroke.

One continuous shape, no counterform to lose. Most gestural and most 'creative tool' of the set.

03

Ember Iris

A true three-blade iris, its blades cut by tangential seams the way real aperture blades overlap, opened onto a triangular core of firelight — the campfire seen straight down the barrel.

The most literally 'camera' mark. Reads as an iris instantly; the triangle keeps it from being a generic lens ring.

04

Hearth Lens

A single flame with a clean circular lens cut through its heart. The hook of the tip keeps it fire rather than a droplet; the void keeps it optics rather than a candle.

One shape, one counterform, one colour. Holds its silhouette at 16 px and is unmistakable on a dark tile.
