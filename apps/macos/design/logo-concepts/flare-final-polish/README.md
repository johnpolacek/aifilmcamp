# AI Film Camp — Flare Final Polish

Three monochromatic refinements of the selected wide Flare mark:

1. **Current** — the selected candidate, preserved as the control.
2. **Balanced** — raises the lens by 6 viewBox units and rounds the flame base to improve optical centering and reduce the map-pin impression.
3. **Aperture C** — uses the Balanced outer shape with a tapered, asymmetric C cutout for a more proprietary camera reference.

## Files

- `ai-film-camp-flare-current.svg`
- `ai-film-camp-flare-balanced.svg`
- `ai-film-camp-flare-aperture-c.svg`
- `ai-film-camp-flare-final-polish.svg` — presentation board

The individual marks use one amber fill (`#F5A623`) and transparent negative space. Change the single path fill to create charcoal, white, or other one-color applications.

## Usage notes

- Use the source SVGs for the app icon and website header.
- Preserve the square viewBox and do not stretch the mark.
- Prefer the normal construction at 32 px and above; inspect a platform-specific micro-icon before shipping below 24 px.
- Keep clear space equal to roughly one quarter of the mark width.
